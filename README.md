# multi-domain-treatment-analysis
Analysis of simulated clinical trial data comparing treatments across multiple outcome domains, including change-from-baseline, preference-weighted scoring, and benefit–risk visualisation.

# Treatment Comparison Across Multiple Domains

This project analyses simulated clinical trial data comparing five treatments across five outcome domains:

- Vital Signs  
- Physical Symptoms  
- Emotional Wellbeing  
- Side Effects  
- Treatment Administration  

## Objective
To evaluate treatment performance across domains and over time, and explore how results change under different patient priorities.

## Approach
- Aggregated mean scores by treatment and domain  
- Calculated change from baseline (Month 6 and Month 12)  
- Built heatmaps for comparison  
- Created weighted scores to reflect different patient preferences  
- Visualised benefit–risk trade-offs  

## Key Insight
No single treatment dominates across all domains — the preferred option depends on which outcomes are prioritised.

## Tools
Python (pandas, seaborn, matplotlib)
